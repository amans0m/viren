Free Ride to Vote.

For seniors, and anyone who needs a ride. We'll come to you. We're not asking who you vote for. We're just asking you to vote.

Advance votingOct 16 to 218 a.m. to 8 p.m.
Final voting daySaturday, Oct 248 a.m. to 8 p.m.

Voting hours: 8 a.m. to 8 p.m.

Before you request

  1. This is a request. Our coordinator will call you to confirm your ride.

Read the full ride rules

Request your free ride

It takes about a minute. This is a request: our coordinator will call you to confirm your ride.

Full ride rules

Rides are run by volunteers, so we keep it fair.

When rides are limited, priority goes to:

  1. Voters with disabilities or mobility challenges who need transportation assistance.
  2. Seniors aged 65 and over who have limited transportation options.
  3. Other voters who need a ride, subject to volunteer availability.

Ride rules

  1. A request is not a confirmed booking. Every request stays pending until our coordinator assigns a driver and confirms your trip with you by phone.
  2. Request at least a day in advance. It gives us time to line up drivers and vehicles.
  3. One return trip per voter. From your home to your voting place and back home. No other stops or destinations, and no cash or reimbursement.
  4. Rides go by need and what we have available. We keep a waitlist and review priority requests first, then offer any remaining space to other voters. If we can't fit you in, we will tell you.
  5. We will call to confirm the details: your pickup address and time, your voting place, any help you need, and whether our volunteer vehicle suits you. Our drivers are volunteers and cannot provide medical care. Please answer calls from unknown numbers.
  6. Please be honest, and tell us if your plans change. To change or cancel, call 778-998-4736. To keep rides fair, we may decline duplicate or unverifiable requests, and repeated no-shows.
  7. Open to everyone, whoever you vote for. We never ask who you vote for, and drivers do not campaign. We never ask for medical details or documents.

Questions, or need to change a request? Call 778-998-4736.
