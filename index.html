Meet Virender.

Virender Dass is a local business owner who calls Abbotsford-Mission home and believes deeply in the strength of family, community, hard work, and personal responsibility. He founded Wellness Extract in 2017, runs SmartVeda, a mental-math and creative-learning centre for children in Abbotsford, and started the Santosh Care Foundation, a registered charity named after his mother.

Living and working here, he has seen the pressures local families are facing, from the rising cost of living and housing to challenges in healthcare and concerns about the direction of our communities. His experience in business has taught him to listen carefully, solve problems practically, and take responsibility for seeing a job through. Those experiences ultimately led him to step forward and seek public office, with the goal of bringing a grounded, community-first approach to representing Abbotsford-Mission.

Where Virender stands.

Virender is running on the OneBC plan. Here it is, issue by issue.

Taxes and the economy

25% off every tax bracket, 2% off the PST, and an audit of 176,000 regulations.

Read the details

Health care

Private care alongside fully funded public care, with spending moved to the front line.

Read the details

Housing

Eliminate the Step Code and block municipal rent control that stops new rentals being built.

Read the details

Education

Standardized testing, SOGI 123 out of classrooms, and a Parental Bill of Rights.

Drugs and addiction

End safe supply, turn consumption sites into recovery centres, and involuntary care for the severely addicted.

Crime and justice

Crack down on repeat and violent offenders, and protect our ports.

When you can vote.

Check that you are registered, then find your voting place and the ID to bring.

Friday to Wednesday Oct 16 to 21 Advance voting

8 a.m. to 8 p.m. at any advance voting place in BC. Places are posted at wheretovote.elections.bc.ca.

Saturday Oct 24 Election day

8 a.m. to 8 p.m. at your assigned voting place. It is listed on your Where to Vote card.

Voting day
Can't get there? Vote by mail Request a package

Request one from Elections BC. Your completed package must reach them by 8 p.m. on October 24.

Who can vote Check you're in Eligibility
