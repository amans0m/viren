Health care and Mission's ER

An emergency room that is open when you need it.

What's wrong

Mission Memorial's emergency department ran reduced hours for 51 nights in a row this spring. In August, Fraser Health announced 16 more overnight closures because of physician staffing. The department flooded in January 2024, and the $18 million expansion that followed began construction in February 2026 and is due to finish in spring 2027.

Mission Record, Aug. 6, 2026Fraser Health

What Virender will do

[Draft for OneBC approval]

  • Make emergency staffing the first priority until the ER keeps regular hours.
  • Move administration and HR spending toward frontline staff and resources, as the OneBC plan proposes.
  • Support private health care alongside fully funded public health care, as the OneBC plan proposes.
  • Publish the ER's hours and closures every month.

Highway 1, Highway 11 and getting around

A commute that matches what was promised.

What's wrong

The NDP's 2020 election platform promised to widen Highway 1 to Abbotsford by 2026. The province now lists the Mt. Lehman to Highway 11 phase for completion in 2031. As of late 2025, the transportation ministry could not give a timeline for the stretch east through Sumas Prairie toward Chilliwack.

Global NewsBC GovernmentCBC

What Virender will do

[Draft for OneBC approval]

  • Press for a public, dated timeline for every remaining phase of Highway 1, including east of Highway 11.
  • Speed up permits and approvals for infrastructure, using the OneBC plan's audit of the province's regulations.
  • Report to residents twice a year on construction progress.

Flood protection for Sumas Prairie

Protection built before the next flood, not after it.

What's wrong

The December 2025 flood was the city's second major flood in four years. In January 2026 the province put $3.3 million toward flood-mitigation planning, and says it has provided about $220 million for recovery, preparedness and mitigation in Abbotsford and Sumas Prairie since 2021. The Abbotsford Chamber of Commerce says the cost of protecting the area is closer to $1.8 billion.

CBCBC Government

What Virender will do

[Draft for OneBC approval]

  • Press for a funded, dated construction plan for Sumas Prairie flood protection.
  • Bring the province, Abbotsford, Chilliwack, local First Nations and the federal government to the table on who pays what.
