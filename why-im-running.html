What running businesses has shown me

I came here in 2014, built a company, and started a learning centre for children with my wife. I have learned how much a province can get in the way of people trying to build something. This is what I see.

  • The province projects a record $13.3 billion deficit this year, and still raised the lowest income-tax rate and widened the PST. The details
  • Nearly 1.3 million British Columbians have no regular family doctor. The details
  • BC's PISA math score fell 42 points between 2003 and 2022. The details
  • More than 18,000 people have died of toxic drugs since 2016. The details

My own example is small but it is real. For a year I have been looking for a building in Abbotsford where my company can package and manufacture, so that the jobs are here and not somewhere else. Serviced industrial land is scarce, permits are slow, and every month of delay is a month of payroll that goes to another province or country. Every business owner in this valley has a version of that story.

What I am running on

I am running on the OneBC plan, because it is the plan I believe in. Cut every tax bracket by 25% and take 2% off the PST. Balance the budget within four years so our children are not paying our interest. Bring back standardized testing, take SOGI 123 and politics out of classrooms, and give parents a Bill of Rights. End safe supply, turn consumption sites into recovery centres, and get the severely addicted into treatment. Eliminate the Step Code so homes cost less to build. Crack down on the repeat offenders who commit most of the crime. Audit 176,000 regulations so people like me can get a permit in months, not years.
