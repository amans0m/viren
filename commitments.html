Commitments.

What Virender promises, and how you can hold him to it.

A promise is only worth something if you can check it. Each commitment below says what it means and how you will be able to see whether it was kept.

Reply to every message within 24 to 48 hours.

What it means

Every email, call or note sent to Virender's office gets a reply from a person within one to two days.

How you can check

Response times will be published on this site every three months.

Hold timely town halls in Mission and Abbotsford.

What it means

Regular in-person town halls in each community, and an extra one whenever a major local issue comes up, with dates announced well ahead.

How you can check

Dates, locations and attendance will be posted here.

Not vote for new provincial consumer taxes.

What it means

Virender will not vote for a new provincial tax on everyday purchases.

How you can check

Every budget vote is recorded in the legislature, and he will explain each one on this site.

Keep a constituency office with published hours.

What it means

A local office residents can walk into, with its hours posted at the door and online.

How you can check

Hours will be on this site, and any change will be posted the same day.

What an MLA can and can't do

Virender will not promise what one MLA cannot deliver. He can promise effort, access, and honesty about what is happening.

An MLA can

  • Vote on laws and on the provincial budget.
  • Question ministers and press ministries on local files.
  • Help residents with provincial services and agencies.
  • Sit on legislative committees and speak for the riding in the House.

An MLA can't

  • Set federal trade policy or tariffs. Those are decided in Ottawa.
  • Run hospitals day to day. Fraser Health does that.
  • Decide municipal matters such as local zoning, city roads or city policing budgets.
  • Spend money or start projects alone. Government decides.
